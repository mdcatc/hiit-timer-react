
# Day 1 Leg & Core Workout

## Warm-Up
- Jumping Jacks: 30s
- High Knees: 30s
- Rest: 60s

## First Circuit (Repeat 3x)
- Squats: 20s
- Glute Bridge March: 20s
- Abdominal Bicycle: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Second Circuit (Repeat 3x)
- Squats Pulses: 20s
- Body Weight Glute Bridge: 20s
- Star Crunches: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Third Circuit (Repeat 3x)
- Sumo Squats Pulses: 20s
- Running In Place: 20s
- Rotating Mountain Climbers: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Cool Down
- Stretch: 120s
