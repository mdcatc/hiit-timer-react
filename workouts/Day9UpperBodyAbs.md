# Day 9 Upper Body & Abs Workout

## Warm-Up

- Jumping Jacks: 30s
- High Knees: 30s
- Rest: 60s
- Start Timer: 5s

## First Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Scapular Push-Ups: 20s
- Pulse Rows: 20s
- Mountain Climbers: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Second Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Diamond Push-Ups: 20s
- Reverse Snow Angels: 20s
- Toe Touches (on your back): 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Third Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Side Kick-Throughs: 20s
- Downward Dog Leg Lifts: 20s
- Roll-Over Crunches: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Cool Down

- Stretch: 120s
