# Day 5 Upper Body & Abs Workout

## Warm-Up

- Jumping Jacks: 30s
- High Knees: 30s
- Rest: 60s
- Start Timer: 5s

## First Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Quadraped Press: 20s
- Contralateral Superman: 20s
- Ab Walkout: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Second Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Inch Worm to Pike Push-Ups: 20s
- Floor T-Raise: 20s
- Russian Twists: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Third Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Tricep Stop Push-Ups: 20s
- Floor Y Raises: 20s
- Plank Up & Down: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Cool Down

- Stretch: 120s
