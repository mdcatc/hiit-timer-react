# Day 4 Leg & Core Workout

## Warm-Up

- Jumping Jacks: 30s
- High Knees: 30s
- Rest: 60s
- Start Timer: 5s

## First Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Butt Kicks: 20s
- Glute Bridge March: 20s
- Oblique Pulse-Ups: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Second Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Squats Pulses: 20s
- Running In Place: 20s
- Heel Taps (Sit Up Position): 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Third Circuit (Repeat 3x)

- Squat Toe Tap: 20s
- Single Leg Glute Bridge Hold: 20s
- Single Leg Scissor Stretch: 20s
- Rest: 60s

## Cool Down

- Stretch: 120s
